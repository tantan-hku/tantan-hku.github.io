Zhuoran Zhang Presents Research on Cognitive Outsourcing at NCTI-DCHK Conference 2025

PhD researcher Zhuoran Zhang from the research group presented ongoing research on cognitive outsourcing to Generative AI and also supported the conference as a student helper.

Location: Department of Real Estate and Construction, HKUEvent: NCTI-DCHK Conference 2025 — Advanced Digital Construction and Innovation in the AI Era

On 15–16 December 2025, PhD researcher Zhuoran Zhang from the research group attended the NCTI-DCHK Conference 2025, Advanced Digital Construction and Innovation in the AI Era, as both a speaker and a student helper.

At the conference, Zhuoran presented her ongoing research, Am I Still the Designer? Modes and Perceived Risks of Cognitive Outsourcing to Generative AI. The study examines the shift from cognitive offloading, in which AI is used to support task efficiency, toward cognitive outsourcing, in which elements of the thinking process itself are delegated to Generative AI. The presentation highlighted emerging concerns related to additional work in managing AI outputs, homogenization of design outcomes, and the potential weakening of designers’ critical thinking and professional agency.

In addition to presenting her research, Zhuoran served as a student helper and supported guest reception and IT arrangements during the conference. The event provided opportunities to exchange ideas with researchers working on digital construction, artificial intelligence, and innovation in the built environment.
