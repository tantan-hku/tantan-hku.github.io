The 2nd International Workshop on Design × Manufacturing

Tan Tan joined manufacturing scholars for a cross-disciplinary discussion on construction design, DfMA, and new opportunities for AI-enabled research.

Time: 14:00–17:30Venue: YEUNG-P7303, City University of Hong KongTheme: Agentic and Generative AI for Design and Manufacturing

On 12 August 2026, Tan Tan attended the Second International Workshop on Design × Manufacturing (DxM) at the Department of Systems Engineering, City University of Hong Kong. He joined the workshop at the invitation of Prof. Jianxi Luo of CityU and Prof. Yi Xiong of the Southern University of Science and Technology.

Tan delivered a presentation titled Challenges and Opportunities of DfMA in Hong Kong’s Construction Industry. He shared design problems and research questions emerging from the construction sector, introduced the research group’s work on Design for Manufacture and Assembly, and exchanged ideas for new projects with scholars working in manufacturing and engineering systems.

Under the theme “Agentic and Generative AI for Design and Manufacturing,” the workshop brought together researchers working on large-language-model-based multi-agent collaboration, finite-element analysis, generative parametric optimization, diffusion models, robotic additive manufacturing, closed-loop process monitoring, and DfMA. The discussions highlighted opportunities to connect construction-specific constraints and knowledge with advances in intelligent manufacturing.

The workshop also included a laboratory visit, providing participants with further opportunities to discuss sensing, robotics, physical experimentation, and potential interdisciplinary collaboration.
