Research Group Attends CRIOCM 2025 in Hangzhou

The research group attended the 30th International Symposium on Advancement of Construction Management and Real Estate (CRIOCM 2025) in Hangzhou, with an invited lecture at Zhejiang University of Technology.

Dates: 7–9 November 2025Conference: CRIOCM 2025 · Zhejiang University City CollegeLecture: Zhejiang University of Technology, School of Design and Architecture

From 7 to 9 November 2025, the research group attended the 30th International Symposium on Advancement of Construction Management and Real Estate (CRIOCM 2025), hosted by Zhejiang University City College (浙大城院).

Members including Tan Tan and Yalan Mei joined the symposium programme and academic exchange with colleagues in construction management and real estate. Alongside the conference, Tan Tan thanks the School of Design and Architecture at Zhejiang University of Technology for inviting him to deliver a guest lecture, Opportunities and Challenges under Digital Design and Construction (数字化设计和建造下的机遇和挑战), on 7 November 2025 (17:00–18:30), sharing perspectives on generative AI, BIM, and robotic construction.
