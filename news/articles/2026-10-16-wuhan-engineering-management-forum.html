Tan Tan Chairs Green and Sustainable Engineering Management Session in Wuhan

At the Engineering Management Youth Academic Forum in Wuhan, Tan Tan chaired Session 4 on green, low-carbon and sustainable engineering management.

Location: Wuhan, ChinaEvent: Engineering Management Youth Academic ForumRole: Chair, Session 4 — Green, Low-Carbon and Sustainable Engineering Management

From 16 to 18 October 2026, Tan Tan attended the Engineering Management Youth Academic Forum in Wuhan. He served as chair of Session 4: Green, Low-Carbon and Sustainable Engineering Management.

The session brought together young scholars to discuss research on green transition, low-carbon practice, and sustainable approaches in engineering management. As session chair, Tan moderated the presentations and academic exchange among participants.
