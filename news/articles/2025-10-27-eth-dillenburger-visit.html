Academic Visit27 October 2025

Research Group Hosts Benjamin Dillenburger from ETH Zurich

The research group hosted Professor Benjamin Dillenburger for a REC research seminar on large-scale additive manufacturing. The visit also included Professor Ming Shan Ng from Kyoto Institute of Technology.

Time: 10:30–11:30Venue: UG02, UG/F, Knowles Building, HKUSeminar: Large-scale Additive Manufacturing – Challenges and Opportunities

On 27 October 2025, the research group hosted Professor Benjamin Dillenburger from ETH Zurich for an academic visit and a Department of Real Estate and Construction (REC) research seminar.

Professor Dillenburger presented Large-scale Additive Manufacturing – Challenges and Opportunities, examining how additive manufacturing is transforming architecture through direct fabrication of complex components, robotic process control, and computational design that balances complexity with efficiency. He leads the Digital Building Technologies research group at ETH Zurich and is known for pioneering work in computational design and digital fabrication.

The visit also welcomed Professor Ming Shan Ng from Kyoto Institute of Technology. Alongside the seminar, the guests joined laboratory exchanges and informal discussions with the research group on digital fabrication, robotics, and design research.
