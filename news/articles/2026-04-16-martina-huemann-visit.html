Research Group Hosts Martina Huemann for Workshop and Industry Visits

The research group hosted Professor Martina Huemann, Editor-in-Chief of the International Journal of Project Management, for a REC research workshop and joint industry visits with EMBA students from WU Vienna.

Workshop: 14:00–16:00, KB529–530, Knowles BuildingTheme: Meet the Editor: How You Get Your Excellent Research PublishedVisits: Light Public Housing, AECOM Asia, The Henderson

On 16 April 2026, the research group hosted Professor Martina Huemann, Editor-in-Chief of the International Journal of Project Management and of Project Leadership and Society. She holds professorships at University College London, where she heads The Project Hub, and at WU Vienna University of Economics and Business.

The group organised an REC research workshop, Meet the Editor: How You Get Your Excellent Research Published, at KB529–530, Knowles Building. Professor Huemann shared editorial perspectives on publishing excellent research, drawing on more than two decades of work in project careers, stakeholder engagement, sustainability, and human resource management in project-oriented organisations. She is a recipient of the IPMA Research Award and the 2025 PMI Research Achievement Award.

Alongside the workshop, the hosts joined Professor Huemann and EMBA students from WU Vienna for industry visits to the Housing Bureau’s new Light Public Housing, AECOM Asia Headquarters, and Henderson Land’s The Henderson, connecting academic exchange with practice in housing, consulting, and landmark development in Hong Kong.
