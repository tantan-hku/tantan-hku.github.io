Academic Visit8–11 September 2026

Academic Exchanges in Nanjing and NSFC Meeting in Wuhu

Tan Tan visited architecture, civil engineering, and engineering management schools in Nanjing before presenting his research at an NSFC project meeting in Anhui.

Locations: Nanjing and Wuhu, ChinaFocus: Generative AI, engineering design, and design management

From 8 to 11 September 2026, Tan Tan undertook a series of academic exchanges in Nanjing and Wuhu. In Nanjing, he visited the School of Engineering Management at Nanjing University; the School of Civil Engineering and the School of Architecture at Southeast University; and the School of Architecture at Nanjing Tech University.

Through presentations and discussions with faculty members and researchers, the visits provided opportunities to exchange ideas on engineering design, design management, digital construction, and emerging applications of generative artificial intelligence.

Tan then travelled to Wuhu, Anhui, to attend the 2026 Young Scientists Fund Project (Category C) Exchange Meeting for the Management Science and Engineering discipline, organized by the Department of Management Sciences of the National Natural Science Foundation of China. He introduced HKU’s Faculty of Architecture and presented an overview of his research on generative AI-based engineering design and management.

The visit connected institutional exchange with focused discussion among young researchers, supporting dialogue on the development, methods, and practical relevance of AI-enabled engineering and management research.
