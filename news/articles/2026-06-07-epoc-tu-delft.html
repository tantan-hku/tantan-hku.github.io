Research Group Presents at EPOC 2026 and Visits TU Delft

Yalan Mei and Zhuoran Zhang presented at the 2026 Engineering Project Organization Conference, Tan Tan joined PhD Day, and the team visited TU Delft.

Dates: 7–10 June 2026Conference: EPOC 2026 — Design for TransformationVisit: TU Delft

From 7 to 10 June 2026, members of the research group attended the 2026 Engineering Project Organization Conference (EPOC 2026), held under the theme Design for Transformation.

Yalan Mei presented the conference paper Effects of AR Embodiment on Cognitive Load and Learning Experience in Robotic Construction Tasks (Mei, Y., Tan, T., Rowlinson, S., Hussain, M. and Sun, K.). The study examines how augmented-reality embodiment shapes cognitive load and learning experience in robotic construction tasks.

Zhuoran Zhang shared the extended abstract Reconfiguring the Design Process through Human–AI Coopetition: Typologies and Interaction Patterns, developed from work on how generative AI influences inspiration and fixation in engineering design processes.

Tan Tan participated in PhD Day sharing with doctoral researchers. Alongside the conference programme, the team also visited TU Delft for academic exchange and informal engagement with colleagues in the Netherlands.
