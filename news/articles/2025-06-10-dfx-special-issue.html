Special Issue: Design for X (DfX) in Digital Construction

Tan Tan is Managing Guest Editor of a special issue in Developments in the Built Environment (Elsevier, IF 8.2), inviting papers on Design for X in digital construction.

Journal: Developments in the Built EnvironmentSubmission open: 10 June 2025Submission deadline: 1 July 2026Acceptance deadline: 1 December 2026

Tan Tan is serving as Managing Guest Editor for the special issue Design for X (DfX) in Digital Construction in Developments in the Built Environment, published by Elsevier (impact factor 8.2). Co-editors are Yu Zhang (University of Cambridge), Ming Shan Ng (Kyoto Institute of Technology), and Chris Rausch (The University of Texas at Austin). Enquiries: tant@hku.hk.

The built environment is undergoing rapid transformation driven by sustainability and efficiency. This special issue focuses on how digital construction, BIM, and construction automation can support design optimisation for multidisciplinary and whole-life-cycle performance. Topics include advanced methods such as generative AI and multimodal learning, and DfX approaches including Design for Manufacture and Assembly (DfMA), Design for Safety, and Design for Sustainability—bridging architecture, construction, and engineering to address industry challenges.

Submissions open on 10 June 2025, with a submission deadline of 1 July 2026 and an acceptance deadline of 1 December 2026.
