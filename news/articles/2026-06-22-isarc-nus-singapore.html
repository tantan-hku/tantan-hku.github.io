Research Group Presents at ISARC 2026 and NUS Seminar

Tan Tan, Zhuoran Zhang, Yalan Mei, and Mudasir Hussain attended ISARC 2026 at the National University of Singapore, presenting new research on automated fall-hazard detection and joining an NUS seminar on digital construction.

Dates: 22–26 June 2026Venue: National University of SingaporeEvents: ISARC 2026 & NUS seminar

From 22 to 26 June 2026, the research group attended the 43rd International Symposium on Automation and Robotics in Construction (ISARC 2026), held together with the 34th Annual Conference of the International Group for Lean Construction at the National University of Singapore.

The team included Tan Tan, Zhuoran Zhang, Yalan Mei, and Mudasir Hussain. At ISARC, they presented the conference paper Integrating RAG-LLMs with BIM for Automated Fall Hazard Detection and Prevention in Building Design (Hussain, M., Tan, T., Zhang, Z. and Yalan, M.). The work explores how retrieval-augmented generation with large language models can be coupled with building information modelling to support automated identification and prevention of fall hazards during building design.

Alongside the conference, Tan Tan delivered an invited seminar presentation, Design for Health and Safety in Digital Construction, as part of the NUS seminar Digital Technologies for Smart and Sustainable Built Environment, hosted by Dr Jinying Xu. The visit also included exchanges with colleagues and a laboratory tour focusing on sensing, robotics, and digital construction technologies.
