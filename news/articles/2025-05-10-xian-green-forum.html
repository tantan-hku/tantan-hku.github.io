Tan Tan Presents at the 2025 Green Engineering Forum in Xi'an

Tan Tan shared research on generative AI-driven engineering design at the forum in Xi'an.

Location: Xi'an, Shaanxi Province, ChinaEvent: 2025 Annual Conference of the Engineering Management Branch of the Management Science and Engineering Society & The First International Academic Forum on Green Engineering and Sustainable Development

On 10–11 May 2025, Tan Tan attended the 2025 Annual Conference of the Engineering Management Branch of the Management Science and Engineering Society and the First International Academic Forum on Green Engineering and Sustainable Development in Xi'an, Shaanxi Province, China.

He delivered a presentation titled Generative AI-driven Engineering Design: Knowledge Co-creation and Human–AI Collaboration, sharing recent work on how generative AI can support knowledge co-creation and human–AI collaboration in engineering design.
