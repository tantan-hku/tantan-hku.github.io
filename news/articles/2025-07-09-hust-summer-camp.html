Academic Visit9–12 July 2025

HKU–HUST Summer Camp on Smart Construction and Smart Cities

Supported by the Ministry of Education Hong Kong–Mainland university exchange programme, Tan Tan led HKU students to Huazhong University of Science and Technology for the 2025 summer camp, with Zhuoran Zhang joining as tutor.

Dates: 9–12 July 2025Host: Huazhong University of Science and Technology, WuhanProgramme: Smart Construction and Smart Cities Summer Camp

From 9 to 12 July 2025, Tan Tan took part in the 2025 Smart Construction and Smart Cities Summer Camp at Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST), supported by the Ministry of Education programme for faculty and student exchange between Hong Kong and mainland universities. He led HKU students to Wuhan for academic exchange, lectures, workshops, and site visits.

The programme included visits to the National Center of Technology Innovation for Digital Construction and the China Architecture Technology Museum, connecting classroom learning with leading digital construction research and practice. Zhuoran Zhang also joined the camp and served as a tutor, supporting student workshops and presentations.

The research group thanks teachers and students at HUST—especially colleagues in the School of Civil and Hydraulic Engineering—for their warm hospitality and generous support throughout the exchange.
