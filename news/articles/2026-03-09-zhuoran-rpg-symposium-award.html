Zhuoran Zhang Receives Poster Presentation Merit Award at HKU Research Postgraduate Symposium 2026

PhD researcher Zhuoran Zhang from the research group participated in the HKU Research Postgraduate Symposium 2026 and received the Poster Presentation Merit Award for presenting her ongoing PhD research.

Location: HKU campusEvent: HKU Research Postgraduate Symposium 2026

On 9 March 2026, PhD researcher Zhuoran Zhang from the research group participated in the HKU Research Postgraduate Symposium 2026 as a recipient of the Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme (HKPFS). At the symposium, she received the Poster Presentation Merit Award for presenting her current PhD research.

Zhuoran presented her ongoing research, From Offloading to Outsourcing: Risks of Cognitive Outsourcing to Generative AI in Design Workflows. The study examines how increasing reliance on Generative AI may shift designers from using AI for cognitive support toward delegating parts of the design thinking process itself.

During the poster session, Zhuoran discussed her research with judges and fellow postgraduate researchers and received questions and constructive feedback. The symposium provided an opportunity to share her latest research progress with the wider HKU research postgraduate community and to further develop the study through academic exchange.
