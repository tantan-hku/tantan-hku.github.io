Zhuoran Zhang Presents at the 2025 Young Scholars Forum on Project Management

PhD researcher Zhuoran Zhang from the research group presented research on how Generative AI influences knowledge conversion and design thinking at the PhD Researchers’ Forum.

Location: Baotou, Inner Mongolia, ChinaEvent: 2025 Young Scholars Forum on Project Management

On 18–19 July 2025, PhD researcher Zhuoran Zhang from the research group attended the 2025 Young Scholars Forum on Project Management in Baotou, Inner Mongolia. The forum was held under the theme AI+ Empowering Innovation and Sustainable Development in Project Management.

At the PhD Researchers’ Forum, Zhuoran delivered a presentation titled Disruption or Co-Creation? The Impacts of Generative AI on Design Knowledge Conversion. The talk introduced her ongoing research on how Generative AI influences architectural design workflows, with particular attention to knowledge conversion and design fixation.

Drawing on interviews with architects, the research highlighted the dual effects of Generative AI on design knowledge conversion and creativity. While GenAI can help designers explore alternatives and potentially break existing design fixation, its outputs may also introduce new forms of fixation that constrain deeper reflection and more original design development. The forum provided an opportunity to discuss these findings with scholars in project management and related fields.
