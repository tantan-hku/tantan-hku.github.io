Managing Design and Construction in the AI Era

CIB W096 brought together international researchers to examine how artificial intelligence is reshaping architectural design, design management, and construction delivery.

Time: 19:00–21:30 HKT (UTC+8)Venue: Online via ZoomOrganizers: Prof. Tan Tan and Prof. Charmaine Ming Shan Ng

On 15 September 2026, the CIB W096 Architectural Design and Management Commission held the online seminar Managing Design and Construction in the AI Era. Prof. Tan Tan of The University of Hong Kong and Prof. Charmaine Ming Shan Ng of Kyoto Institute of Technology jointly organized the event.

Speakers from universities in Europe, Asia, and Hong Kong discussed knowledge architecture, evidence-informed programming, construction-waste reuse, design knowledge management, AI-supported construction delivery, semantic modelling for human–robot collaboration, and the changing practice of design management. Together, the talks considered how AI can support decision-making and production while keeping human knowledge, governance, and collaboration at the centre.

PhD researcher Zhuoran Zhang from the research group assisted with seminar coordination and delivered a presentation titled Reconfiguring the Design Process through Human–AI Coopetition and Knowledge Co-creation. Her talk examined how competitive and collaborative relations between humans and AI may reshape design processes and the creation of design knowledge.

The seminar was supported by the journals Engineering Management and Developments in the Built Environment, including the special issue “Design for X in Digital Construction.”
