Mei Yalan Presents at the 3rd Construction Safety Symposium

PhD researcher Mei Yalan from the research group shared her work at the graduate forum and contributed a question during the roundtable discussion.

Location: School of Civil Engineering, Tsinghua UniversityEvent: 3rd Construction Safety Academic Symposium and Graduate Forum

On 11–12 September 2026, PhD researcher Mei Yalan from the research group attended the Third Construction Safety Academic Symposium of the Chinese Research Institute of Construction Management and its Graduate Forum at Tsinghua University.

At the graduate forum, she presented her research on the Applicability Evaluation of Urban Construction Methods Based on Multi-source Spatial Data and Engineering Constraints. She also participated in the roundtable question session, exchanging views with attending experts and scholars on current construction-safety research.

The symposium was hosted by the Chinese Research Institute of Construction Management and Tsinghua University, and organized by Tsinghua University’s School of Civil Engineering. More than 100 experts, scholars, industry representatives, and young researchers from construction safety management, intelligent construction, urban resilience and safety, and related fields attended. Participants discussed emerging research questions and practical challenges in construction safety.
