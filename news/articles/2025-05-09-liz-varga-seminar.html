Research Group Hosts Liz Varga CBE for Infrastructure Seminar

The research group hosted Professor Liz Varga CBE for a talk and research-group visit at the Department of Real Estate and Construction, and supported her Visiting Professor Scheme appointment for a one-year exchange.

Date: May 2025Venue: Department of Real Estate and Construction, HKUSeminar: Infrastructure: A systems approach

In May 2025, the research group hosted Professor Liz Varga CBE for a seminar and research-group visit at the Department of Real Estate and Construction (REC), The University of Hong Kong.

Professor Varga is Professor of Complex Systems at University College London (UCL), affiliated with the Infrastructure Systems Institute and the Civil, Environmental, Geomatic Engineering Department. She delivered Infrastructure: A systems approach, sharing a systems perspective on infrastructure research and practice.

The research group also helped Professor Varga obtain Visiting Professor Scheme funding. She serves as Visiting Professor for one year to exchange with the group on infrastructure systems and related research.
