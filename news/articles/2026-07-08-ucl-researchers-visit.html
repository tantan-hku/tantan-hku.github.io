Research Group Hosts Three Visiting Scholars from UCL

Stergios Aristoteles Mitoulis, Weiwei Chen, and Yiming Xiang visited the research group for two seminars and an exchange on resilient, sustainable infrastructure.

Time: 10:30–12:00Venue: KB526, 5/F, Knowles Building, HKUFormat: REC Research Seminar

On 8 July 2026, the research group welcomed Prof. Stergios Aristoteles Mitoulis, Dr Weiwei Chen, and Yiming Xiang from University College London for an academic visit, two research seminars, and an extended discussion with faculty members and researchers at HKU.

Prof. Mitoulis presented Smart, Sustainable, and Resilient: A New Paradigm for Cities Reconstruction After Extensive Breakdowns. Drawing on infrastructure exposed to climate extremes, accidents, resource constraints, and cascading disruptions, the talk examined compound hazards and systemic resilience. It highlighted recovery resources, infrastructure interdependencies, and assets that are vital to resilience, with implications for disaster-risk reduction and sustainable infrastructure policy.

Dr Chen presented AI-Enabled Digital Twins for Sustainable Infrastructure: Hydropower System and Heritage Buildings. Her seminar explored how sensor data, Internet of Things systems, real-time monitoring, and advanced analytics can support predictive maintenance and performance optimization. Examples from hydropower infrastructure and heritage assets illustrated applications in fault detection, resilience, carbon reduction, and conservation.

Yiming Xiang joined the visit and discussion. The exchange connected infrastructure resilience, digital twins, asset management, and data-driven decision support, while opening further opportunities for collaboration between the visiting scholars and the research group.
